# AETHRIVA

AETHRIVA is a public, buildless static frontend demonstration that brings synthetic wellness observations into a coherent timeline and makes the calculations behind a finding inspectable. Its audience includes public hackathon judges and visitors exploring the product without an account.

The fictional workspace belongs to Alex Taylor. One fixed October 1–30, 2026 fixture, interpreted in America/Los_Angeles, supplies all seven main views: Overview, Connect, Recovery, Insights, Timeline, Intelligence, and Reports. About explains provenance, limitations, and authorship.

Intelligence supports five deterministic question intents using local browser calculations and complete input evidence. Filters, selected dates, comparisons, charts, and reports reuse the same records. Missing values remain absent; observed zero remains zero. Associations require seven paired observations and carry small-sample warnings below fourteen. Results describe recorded data; they do not establish cause, diagnose, predict disease, prescribe treatment, or provide medical advice.

There are no real connectors, backend, accounts, uploads, or live language-model calls. Source/import displays are fictional demonstration context. Real imports, clinical records, and sharing are future possibilities, not implemented capabilities. No personal data is required to explore the public demo.

The selected visual identity is the existing Carbon + Nunito world, documented in DESIGN.md. The public build is dist/; the approved frontend-first scope in docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md supersedes backend and provider instructions in preserved reference documents. docs/SOURCE_MAP.md records reuse and provenance.
